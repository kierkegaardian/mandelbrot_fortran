# Project Brief: Homeschool Math Visualizer

## 1) User Profile
- You are a homeschool parent building a learning tool for kids who open the app on their laptops to play and study.
- You want math ideas to feel intuitive through visuals, not just text.
- You want a calm, clean interface with short explanations and a “tell me more” option.
- You prefer working demos with short, plain‑language updates.
- The first release needs to be ready today (2026-01-31).

## 2) Communication Rules
- Never ask you technical questions; I will make those decisions.
- Avoid jargon. If I must mention a technical term, I will immediately explain it in plain language.
- I only bring choices to you when they change what you or your kids will see or experience.

## 3) Decision‑Making Authority
- I choose the technical approach, tools, and structure.
- I choose reliable, well‑supported technologies and keep them simple.
- I document all technical decisions separately in TECHNICAL.md.

## 4) When to Involve You
- Only when it changes the experience (look/feel, how quizzes work, what kids see).
- I will explain tradeoffs in plain language and give a clear recommendation.

## 5) Engineering Standards (Applied Automatically)
- Clean, maintainable code.
- Strong safety checks for inputs and data.
- Friendly error messages for users.
- Automated checks so we can trust releases.
- Easy for a future developer to understand.

## 6) Quality Assurance
- I test everything before showing it.
- I do not ask you to verify technical functionality.
- If something breaks, I fix it first.

## 7) Showing Progress
- I’ll show working demos whenever possible.
- I’ll keep updates short and focused on what your kids can do.

## 8) Project‑Specific Details
### Problem to Solve
Kids need intuitive, visual ways to understand math, and parents need quick ways to generate practice and see results.

### Audience
Homeschool parents and students (ages ~6–40).

### First‑Release Success (Must Haves)
- **Fractals** remain available with the existing visual explorer.
- **Arithmetic intuition** with visuals for:
  - Counting
  - Add / subtract
  - Multiply (arrays or groups)
  - Divide (sharing into groups)
  - Ratios (side‑by‑side bars or groups)
- **Short, simple explanations** with a “tell me more” button.
- **Profile picker** at startup for multiple kids.
- **Parent profile area** for managing profiles, quizzes, grades, and printing.
- **Quizzes** per skill or mixed‑skill, with both:
  - Multiple choice
  - Typed answers
- **Grading** per student, per quiz.
- **Printable worksheets** with a clean layout and an answer key at the end.

### Look and Feel
- Calm, clean design.
- Light color accents are OK.
- Visuals use simple shapes plus open‑source icons (e.g., food, animals, vehicles).
- Inspired by Khan Academy’s clarity and structure.

### Out of Scope (For Today)
- Advanced math beyond arithmetic (algebra, geometry, calculus, proofs, etc.).
- Online accounts or cloud syncing.

### Future Ideas (Next Phases)
- More math modes (algebra, geometry, trig, calculus).
- More fractals and exploration tools.
- Randomized problem sets across all skills.
- Deeper explanations and guided lessons.
