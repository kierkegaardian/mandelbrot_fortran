Who this is for right now

Best fit today

  • Homeschool families who want offline-first math review.
  • Parents who want profiles, assignments, and printable practice in one place.
  • Learners who benefit from visual explanations and gentle repetition.

Not the primary fit yet

  • Students expecting a browser-first or mobile-first experience.
  • Families needing a course-complete geometry textbook or complete Algebra 2 coverage today.

Why the launch is homeschool-first

The current app already has the shape homeschool families expect: local profiles, parent controls, printable worksheets, assignments, dashboards, and a calm desktop workflow. College review is a later market because that audience expects web or mobile access, explicit placement-test positioning, and quicker cross-device onboarding.

Short version: MandelQuest is launchable as a homeschool-family beta once trust assets are in place. It is not yet ready as a cold-start college math-review product.

MandelQuest is a local-first desktop math practice app powered by a Modern Fortran rendering engine and a Python Tkinter interface.
