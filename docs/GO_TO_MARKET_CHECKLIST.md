# Go-To-Market Checklist

## Homeschool-first launch
- Position public copy around: `Offline-first, intuition-first math practice for homeschool families.`
- Keep fractals as a secondary exploration bonus, not the public headline.
- Publish and maintain these public pages:
  - `docs/index.html`
  - `docs/coverage.html`
  - `docs/privacy.html`
  - `docs/support.html`
  - `docs/homeschool-beta.html`
  - `docs/first-15-minutes.html`
  - `docs/sample-worksheet.html`
- Keep the support funnel active:
  - GitHub issue templates for bugs and beta feedback
  - Clear support-guide link before people file reports
- Do not start review outreach until:
  - Packaging is stable
  - Coverage honesty is explicit
  - Backup/support docs are live
  - Real family feedback exists

## Beta operations
- Recruit 15 to 25 homeschool families.
- Prioritize local co-ops, personal networks, and homeschool groups in that order.
- Ask for at least two weeks of use, not just install feedback.
- Collect:
  - 5 to 10 written testimonials
  - 3 short screen-recorded reactions
  - install-friction notes
  - “would you pay for this?” feedback

## Paid launch gate
- Signed Windows and macOS installers
- Public launch pages live
- Sample worksheet ready to share
- Beta feedback issue path active
- At least 3 external review or listing placements in progress

## Pricing default
- Closed beta: free
- Paid launch: `$59` one-time household license
- Refund window: 14 days

## College/adult rule
- Do not market broadly to college students yet.
- Revisit only after:
  - web/mobile access exists
  - adult-facing onboarding exists
  - placement-review positioning exists
  - pilot outcome evidence exists
