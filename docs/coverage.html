Coverage matrix

This page is intentionally direct. Families should know where the app is already strong, where it is selective, and where it is not yet the right tool.

Area Current depth Best current use Not yet a full promise
Foundations and arithmetic Strong Daily review, visual intuition, worksheets, parent-managed practice Full standards mapping by state or publisher
Pre-algebra Strong to moderate Mixed operations, ratios, early algebra readiness A complete pre-algebra course replacement
Algebra 1 Moderate Review, reinforcement, and assignment-based practice A full semester-by-semester textbook replacement
Algebra 2 Selective Targeted review where current generators or templates exist Broad course-complete Algebra 2 coverage
Geometry Selective Area/perimeter style review and worksheet practice Proof-heavy geometry or a full geometry track
Statistics Selective but truthful Integrated descriptive statistics and probability review An AP Statistics or full intro statistics course
Trig Narrow Right-triangle trig ratio review A broad trigonometry curriculum
Calculus Narrow Targeted refresh on a few explicit patterns AP Calculus or college calculus replacement coverage
SAT / PSAT / GRE math Targeted review Focused mixed-domain practice and local test-prep review A browser-first adaptive prep platform
Fractal lab Strong Math exploration and visual enrichment The main reason most homeschool families will buy the app

Best-fit family profile

Families looking for calm, local-first review across foundations through Algebra 1, with some selective later-topic refresh and printable practice.

Wrong-fit family profile

Families needing a browser-native full curriculum with comprehensive Geometry, Algebra 2, or college-placement alignment on day one.

Positioning default

Use this sentence publicly: “MandelQuest is an offline-first, intuition-first math practice app for homeschool families.”
