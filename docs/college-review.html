College and adult math review is a later market.

MandelQuest has ingredients that can matter to adult learners, but it is not yet positioned or delivered like a strong cold-start college review product.

What already helps

  • Local quiz engine and progress tracking
  • Targeted SAT, PSAT, and GRE math review
  • Calm practice flow for people rebuilding confidence

Why it is not the first public market

  • Desktop installer is the main entry point
  • No explicit ACCUPLACER or TSIA2 track yet
  • No web/mobile try-now path
  • No adult-first onboarding and no institutional reporting layer

What has to exist before a college pilot

  • Separate adult-facing landing page and onboarding path
  • Explicit placement-review tracks for college algebra, trig, or placement prep
  • Web-based or mobile-friendly access
  • Outcome evidence from a placement-aligned or adult-learner pilot
Default rule: do not market MandelQuest broadly to college students until the product has a fast cross-device entry path and an outcome-specific promise.

Today’s public launch focus remains the homeschool family beta.
