Closed homeschool beta

The first launch wave is a closed beta for 15 to 25 homeschool families in the U.S. The goal is not downloads alone. The goal is real home use over two weeks.

Request beta access See the onboarding path

What beta families get

  • Free beta access
  • Direct influence on wording, trust assets, and workflow friction
  • Early access to family-facing docs and sample materials
  • A clear support path for install, backup, and quiz issues

What the beta needs from families

  • At least two weeks of real use
  • At least one child or learner session plus one parent-management session
  • Written feedback about friction, trust, and usefulness
  • Optional permission for a public quote or short recorded reaction

Success gates for the homeschool launch

Gate Target
Active families 20 active families
Install success 70% or better without live setup help
Weekly activity 60% or better over 4 weeks
Public proof 5 usable testimonials and 3 short recorded reactions
Support load Fewer than 1 support thread per 10 installs on average

Recruitment order

  1. Local co-ops and known homeschool families
  2. Personal-network referrals
  3. Homeschool Facebook groups after trust assets are polished
  4. Review and directory outreach only after the app is review-ready

Paid launch default

After the beta, the default pricing model is a one-time household license at $59 with a 14-day refund window.

That keeps pricing aligned with the app’s current local-first shape and avoids promising cloud-service value that does not exist yet.

Review-readiness rule

Do not start broad review outreach yet. The app should first have a stable public landing page, clear coverage honesty, support docs, and beta feedback proof.

Need to evaluate the family experience first? Start with the sample worksheet and first 15 minutes guide.
