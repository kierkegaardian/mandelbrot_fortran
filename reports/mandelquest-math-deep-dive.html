5. What We're Doing Wrong

14 concrete, file-tied items focused on barriers to model-assisted authoring of correct multi-layer math content and runes.

1. No model entry point for runes (app/question_bank.py:23, scripts/*) — everything is manual DB/JSON insert.
2. Runes only in opaque SQLite + generated manifests (db.py:181). No source-controlled catalog.
3-6. Safe-eval narrow; fractal iteration hardcoded in Fortran; precision fragile; dual generator paths with silent fallback.
7-14. Summer long-form not model-driven; layers siloed; no parent authoring layer; no rune editor/preview; story/mode not rune-aware; packaging hides surface; no asset validation; imperative generators easy to hallucinate in.
Root cause: render + quiz-engine first. Rune authoring was never first-class for models.
